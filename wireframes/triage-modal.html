Design notes

  1. Exact matches never appear. They are applied silently and reported only as a count. The modal is an exception queue, not a list of everything in the file. If the seed profile covers the whole baseline, state 1 is what the team sees — one line and a button.
  2. The plain-English line is the primary control surface. Under time pressure people classify from that sentence and nothing else. Raw fields stay collapsed. Budget real effort on this wording.
  3. Role and disposition are separate dropdowns. Role drives validators, disposition drives output. Folding them together makes "this is the scoring rule, but regenerate it" inexpressible.
  4. Structural matches state the delta in words, not as a diff. The Remote_Access row is the scenario that matters: Green Team rotates the VPN range between years, and a tool that presented it as "unknown rule" would invite someone to drop it and lock the team out.
  5. Lockout-critical requires typing the name. Not a checkbox — checkboxes get clicked reflexively.
  6. Referential integrity blocks, it does not warn. State 4 shows an alias marked drop while a kept rule still references it. Continue is disabled until resolved.
  7. Accept-all is available in state 2, absent in state 3. There is no safe bulk answer for items the tool has never seen.
