Design notes

  1. Every step is completable by typing. Paste is an accelerator, never a requirement — the annex format will change between revisions and the paste will sometimes fail. A wizard that only works when the paste works is a wizard that fails on the morning it matters.
  2. Parses render back for confirmation. Silent mis-parse is the failure mode to design against, not parse failure. Failure is visible; a wrong subnet is not.
  3. Out-of-Bounds hosts are surfaced loudly at step 3, because scoringbot and npc-server sit inside the workstation segment and appear on no range diagram. Tightening that segment without knowing is how teams kill their own scoring from the inside.
  4. Step 4 is the highest-value screen in the tool. The annexes contain no port numbers; the ISA board contains all of them. Defaults come from isa-checks.yaml, the operator confirms against the live board.
  5. Confidence is shown, not hidden. standard assumed unverified — allow-all on an unknown service is the right day-one call, and the wrong day-three one. Visibility is what makes the difference.
  6. Step 5 is confirmation, not authoring. §2.6 is Green Team's stated baseline. The operator validates it; original thinking belongs in step 6.
  7. Step 6 carries the warning because a bare egress deny is where teams historically break Elastic, the NPC server's scored outbound path, and any Graynet Access check — all silently.
